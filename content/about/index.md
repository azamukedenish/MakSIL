Makerere Safe Intelligence Lab (MakSIL) focuses on AI safety and alignment, model evaluation, robustness and security, responsible AI, AI governance, and AI safety in African contexts.

## Our research direction

We aim to contribute to a future in which AI systems are safe, reliable, and responsive to the people and communities they affect.

## An African perspective

African languages, social contexts, and deployment environments raise important questions for AI safety. MakSIL places these questions at the centre of its research direction.
