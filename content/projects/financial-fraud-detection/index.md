This project uses MoMTSim to simulate mobile money transactions and known fraud scenarios in Sub-Saharan Africa. It compares six machine learning classifiers on the resulting synthetic datasets, evaluating how well they identify fraudulent transactions in imbalanced data. The research considers both detection performance and computational demands to inform model selection for mobile money platforms.
