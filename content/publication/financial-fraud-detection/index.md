---

The study uses MoMTSim to simulate mobile money transactions and fraudulent situations, then evaluates six classifiers using precision, recall, F1-score, AUC-ROC, and Matthews correlation coefficient. It compares detection quality with computational demands to inform model selection for mobile money platforms.
